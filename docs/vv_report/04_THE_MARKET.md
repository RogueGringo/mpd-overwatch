# 04. The Market

The Permian Basin produces 6 million barrels per day from
approximately 30,000 active horizontal wells. Operators
drill roughly 4,000 new wells per year in the Delaware
and Midland sub-basins combined.

Almost none of them use MPD.

They drill with 13+ ppg mud into overpressured Wolfcamp
that has 11.5 ppg pore pressure. They accept 800+ psi
of overbalance as normal. They lose mud into depleted
parent-well fractures. They drill through narrow pressure
windows by adding LCM and hoping.

They complete every well the same way: uniform stage spacing,
uniform cluster count, uniform proppant loading from heel
to toe. They do not know which stages are in depleted rock,
which are in overpressured sweet spots, which are drilling
through natural fractures. The data to know this exists
in the MWD and EDR files. Nobody reads it for completions.

The arithmetic:

  4,000 wells/year
  x $150,000 MPD service per well
  = $600 million/year addressable service revenue

  4,000 wells/year
  x 140,000 BOE additional recovery per well
  x $70/BOE
  = $39.2 billion/year in production value created

The service cost is 0.4% of the value created.

The question is not whether operators can afford MPD.
The question is whether they can afford to keep
leaving $10 million per well in the ground.

The company that shows them the math wins the market.

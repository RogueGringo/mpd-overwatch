# 03. The Craft

> VISION DOCUMENT. This describes the operational philosophy, not computed results.
> Zone classifications shown are illustrative examples of platform capability.
> Actual classifications require well-specific MWD/EDR data as input.

There are two ways to do MPD.

**The rental way:**
Ship the choke manifold. Send a technician. Hook it up.
Run the job. Ship it to the next rig. Send the invoice.
The operator gets equipment. The value depends on who
happens to be running it that day.

**The craft way:**
Study the offset wells. Compute the pressure window.
Design the mud weight and SBP program. Know the formation
before the bit touches it. Run the job with a plan that
adapts in real time based on what the well tells you.
After TD, hand the completions engineer a map of the
lateral showing where to put clusters and where to use
diverter. After production, compare the forecast to actual
and calibrate for the next well.

The difference is not the equipment. It is the engineering.

This platform is the engineering.

It takes the data that MPD generates - the APWD readings,
the choke pressure trends, the flow balance, the gamma
correlation - and turns it into decisions:

  At 13,500 ft: APWD dropped 280 psi below baseline.
  Flow ratio: 0.93. ROP increased 40%.
  Classification: depleted zone.
  Action: reduce proppant in stages 15-17.
  Basis: frac fluid will leak into pre-existing fractures.

  At 15,800 ft: APWD rose 400 psi above baseline.
  Choke pressure increasing 0.5 psi/ft.
  Classification: overpressured interval.
  Action: add clusters in stages 26-28. Increase pump rate.
  Basis: high reservoir energy. Maximum contact area warranted.

This is what Allen Hensley has been doing by instinct and
experience for 30 years. The platform encodes that judgment
in mathematics so it scales beyond one consultant, one well,
one phone call at a time.

The craft does not change. The reach does.

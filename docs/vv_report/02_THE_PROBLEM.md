# 02. The Problem

The MPD industry has a positioning problem.

Every sales call starts with: "What does your equipment cost per day?"
Every bid is evaluated on: "Who is cheapest?"

This happens because nobody shows the operator what MPD
actually does to their well. Not in general terms. Not in
case studies from the North Sea. In specific, computable,
verifiable terms for the well they are about to drill.

The operator sees a line item: MPD service, $150,000.

The operator does not see:
- 819 psi of overbalance pushing mud into their pay zone
- Skin factor of 3.26 choking flow into the wellbore
- 70% of their frac clusters doing the work while 30% sit idle
- 140,000 barrels of oil left in the ground

Because nobody computes it for them.

This platform computes it.

For any well. With any formation. At any oil price.
Traceable to the equations. Verifiable by hand.

The operator's question changes from "What does MPD cost?"
to "What does NOT using MPD cost?"

The answer, for a Delaware Basin Wolfcamp well, is
ten million dollars.

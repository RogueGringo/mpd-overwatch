Thesis

MPD is a craft technical
operation, not equipment rental

The industry treats managed pressure drilling as commodity rental: ship equipment, send a technician, collect an invoice. This platform proves MPD is engineering. The results of the technology matter more than the price of the service.

01 — The Craft

30 Years of Judgment, Computed

The rental model: ship equipment, send technician, collect invoice. The craft model: study offsets, design pressure program, adapt in real-time from MWD data, inform completions, calibrate against post-production. This platform encodes Allen Hensley's 30-year MPD methodology into repeatable mathematics. Every equation has a source. Every recommendation has a derivation.

02 — The Market

4,000 Wells/Year. $39B Value.

4,000 wells/year in the Permian Basin. Most drill with 13+ ppg mud into 11.5 ppg pore pressure: 800+ psi overbalance. Arithmetic: 140,000 BOE uplift per well at $70/bbl = $9.8M additional revenue. Service cost: $150,000. Ratio: 65:1. Total addressable: $600M/year service revenue against $39.2B/year production value created. MPD service cost = 0.4% of value created.

03 — The Future

Compounding Data Advantage

Well 1: published literature, wide uncertainty. Well 5: basin-calibrated transport maps, tighter predictions. Well 20: statistical confidence from field-specific data. Well 100: proprietary behavior models that no competitor can replicate. The system learns from every deployment. The competition stays static. 30 years of Hensley knowledge scales beyond one phone call.
